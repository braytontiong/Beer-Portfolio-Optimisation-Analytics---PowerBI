# Beer Portfolio Optimization Analytics

## Overview

Analysed beer sales and product portfolio performance for a national beer distributor to identify opportunities for revenue growth and portfolio optimisation.

## Business Problem

The company offers a large portfolio of beer products across multiple regions and sales channels but lacks visibility into which products drive revenue, profitability, and long-term business value.

## Key Insight

Analysis revealed that a relatively small group of products generated a disproportionate share of total revenue and profit, while several products consistently underperformed across multiple markets.

## Recommendation

Recommended prioritising investment and marketing resources toward high-performing products while reviewing low-performing products for repositioning, targeted promotions, or potential discontinuation.

## Impact

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard that enables management to evaluate product performance, profitability, and regional demand patterns to support data-driven portfolio decisions.
