# Beer Portfolio Optimization Analytics

## Overview

Analysed **beer sales and product portfolio performance** for a national beer distributor to identify opportunities for revenue growth and portfolio optimisation.

## Business Problem

Despite generating **$4.31M in annual revenue** , the company experienced a **0.4% decline** in year-over-year growth and inconsistent profitability across products, regions, and sales channels. Management needed greater visibility into the **factors** driving profit performance and portfolio effectiveness.

The company offers **a large portfolio of beer products** across multiple regions and sales channels but lacks **visibility** into which products drive revenue, profitability, and long-term business value.

## Key Insight

 • Identified IPAs, Lagers, and Wheat Beers as the **strongest profit drivers**, generating margins between 44% and 47%.
 • Found that **aggressive discounting** reduced margins to approximately 35% without sufficient volume growth to offset profitability losses.
  • Discovered that Mountain and Southwest **regions** consistently **underperformed** across both revenue and profit metrics.
 • Revealed **a clear channel trade-off**: off-premise channels drove volume while on-premise channels generated higher margins.
 
## Recommendation
We identified several business red flags that management was not seeing, then provided a dashboard and recommendations to address them.
 • **Prioritise high-margin beer families** through increased promotional support and shelf allocation.
 • Replace blanket discounting with **targeted tiered pricing and loyalty-based incentives**.
 • Conduct **operational reviews** in underperforming regions to identify pricing, distribution, or product mix issues.
 • **Optimise channel strategies** by focusing on volume growth in off-premise channels and margin expansion in on-premise channels.
 
## Impact

Developed an **interactive Power BI dashboard** that provides management with visibility into product performance, profitability, regional demand patterns, customer segments, and channel effectiveness.

Based on these findings, recommendations were developed to prioritise high-margin beer families, redesign discount strategies, optimise channel positioning, and investigate operational issues within underperforming regions.

The dashboard enables management to **continuously monitor these risk areas**, identify emerging performance issues, and make evidence-based portfolio optimisation decisions.
